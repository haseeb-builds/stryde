Outcome-Driven Execution

What are you trying to make happen?

Stryde keeps a persistent operational record of your pursuit. Instead of generating endless advice, it isolates the current bottleneck, commits to one concrete next move, and separates self-reported progress from verified results.

How a pursuit operatesTruthful state model
1. BottleneckDiagnose what is actually blocking the outcome right now before proposing work.
2. Next MoveChoose one concrete action to execute or delegate—never a wall of unprioritized tasks.
3. VerificationKeep user-reported updates distinct from source-backed or independently verified outcomes.
